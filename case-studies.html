CASE STUDY FORMAT

Every project story should answer four questions.

01

What was the challenge?

Material, component, service condition, welding process or quality requirement.

02

What was supplied?

Product family, grade, application method and supporting technical input.

03

What changed?

Weld quality, serviceability, production consistency, maintenance or project delivery impact.

04

What can be learned?

Technical insight that helps similar customers make better welding decisions.

Case-study publishing control

Detailed project names, customer names, technical values, savings, performance claims and photographs should only be published after WAPL approval and customer permission where required.
