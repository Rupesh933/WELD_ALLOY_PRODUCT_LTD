Stainless Steel Electrodes

WA 316L

AWS A5.4 E316L-16

Rutile coated, low carbon electrode with molybdenum for welding 316 and 316L stainless steels. Gives a smooth arc, easy slag removal and good resistance to corrosion.

Overview

Product details

WA 316L deposits a weld metal with low carbon content, which helps prevent carbide precipitation and intergranular corrosion. The molybdenum content improves resistance to pitting in chloride and acidic environments. Redry the electrodes before use and keep them in a heated quiver at site.
